Our Heritage

A little love, in every layer.

HONE Bakes began around a warm home kitchen island—born from a shared mother-daughter devotion to meticulous baking, gentle sweetness, and the tranquil ritual of Japanese tea.

What started as an intimate home-baking journey quickly resonated across Singapore. Today, from our micro-bakery studio at EON Shenton, we continue to craft each bake by hand: honouring slow fermentation, honest textures, and ingredients that speak softly for themselves.

“We don’t believe in rushing the crumb or drowning authentic tea in sugar. True luxury is balance.”

The Art of Slow Baking

Balance, Patience & Japanese Simplicity

In a world of artificial flavorings and towering sugary frostings, we choose restraint. We celebrate the natural bitterness of shaded tea leaves, the deep nuttiness of toasted sesame, and the comforting chew of wild sourdough starters.

01 / TEA
